The Pistachio & Burrata, and how it got on the menu

The Pistachio & Burrata began as a one-weekend special and never left. It is built on the usual San Marzano base with fior di latte, then finished with Sicilian pistachio cream, fresh burrata, basil oil and a little lemon zest.

The burrata goes on after the pizza comes out of the oven, so it stays cool and creamy against the hot crust. The lemon zest cuts through the richness, which is why we add it last and never before.

Please note it is made with pistachios and dairy. If you have an allergy, tell your server before you order, and we will talk you through the rest of the menu. If you would rather skip the nuts, the Margherita and the Quattro Formaggi are good places to start.
